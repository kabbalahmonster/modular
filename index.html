A patchable synthesizer, drum machine and sequencer that lives entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is downloaded. Audio needs a click to start — pick a patch:

quick keys

  • Space play/stop · F fit view · H help · L library · Del delete selection · Ctrl+D duplicate
  • Drag from any output jack → input jack (audio/CV) or any knob (modulation)
  • Drag an input jack away to unplug · right-click a cable to cut it

BOUNCE AUDIO

Renders a hidden copy of your rack faster than real time. If song mode is on, the arrangement is used. The master file uses the current master volume and is limited to 10 minutes. Stems are taken from each OUT module after its Level/Pan, before its limiter and the master bus.

HELP / PATCHING

signals

  • Round jacks = audio/CV signals. Square jacks = triggers/gates (events).
  • CV is normalised −1 … +1. Pitch CV: 1.0 = +48 semitones, so a Sequencer Pitch out into an Oscillator Pitch knob at depth 1.00 tracks 1:1.
  • Exponential destinations (filter cutoff, osc pitch, LFO rate) are modulated in cents.

modulation

  • Drop a cable on a knob to modulate it. A yellow ring shows the mod range, the dot shows the live value.
  • Alt+drag a modulated knob to trim the depth of its newest cable, or click the cable and use the depth slider in the bottom bar (negative = inverted).
  • Mod sources: Envelope, LFO, Sequencer (pitch/vel), or any audio output (audio-rate FM/AM!).

mouse

  • Knobs: drag (Shift = fine), wheel, double-click = reset, right-click = remove newest mod cable.
  • Jacks: drag to patch, right-click to unplug everything on that jack.
  • Cables: click to select/edit depth, right-click to cut.
  • Module: drag header to move, ⧉ duplicate, 🎲 randomise, ▾ collapse, ✕ delete.
  • Canvas: drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Palette icons can be clicked or dragged in.
  • Sequencer: pick a lane (ON/PITCH/VEL/GATE/PROB/RATCH/SLIDE) then drag across the grid. Shift+drag mutes steps.
  • Select: click a header, Shift/Ctrl+click to add, Shift+drag on the canvas for a marquee, Ctrl+A for all. Drag any selected header to move the group.
  • Edit: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · Ctrl+C/X/V copy/cut/paste (works across tabs) · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+Shift+D duplicate with cables
  • Play: K piano keys (Z/X octave) · MIDI button for a controller · learn then click a knob and move a CC · SEQ ● rec records what you play.
  • Song: S opens the arranger. Patterns A–H per SEQ; click a letter to switch at loop end (Shift = now). bounce renders audio faster than real time (optional stems).
